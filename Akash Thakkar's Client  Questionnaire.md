Asking questions of your clients is one of the most important things you can do as a
freelancer. A good question can turn a client who's on the fence into one who's ready to
pay you.
Note that a lot of these questions have to do with the people behind the project, not just
the project itself. That's key. No one wants to hire a composer/sound designer. They
want to hire at trusted expert who can stick with them through all the ups and downs of
the process.
Here are some of the most effective questions I've found to ask potential clients during
your first call with them.
1. How big is the team that you'd be working with?
2. Have they made games before? Have they worked with someone like you before?
3. Do they have a budget? What's set aside for sound/music?
4. Is that budget set in stone?
5. What are their time frames?
6. How much sound/music will they need?
7. What are their sources of funding?
8. Is this game being shown off at festivals/conferences?
9. How long can you expect to wait for feedback between each delivery?
10. What are their expectations for the game?
11. What are their hopes, dreams, and fears for the project?
12. How did they find you?
13. Why hire someone like you right now? Why not wait?
14. What are their policies on crunch?
15. Why do they need music/sound/whatever it is you do?
16. Why not do nothing at all or just get the sound/music for free from a website or a
student?
